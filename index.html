    How to play

    1. Pass the phone round. Everyone adds their name and picks a secret card.
    2. Everyone gets the same word, except the imposter, whose card is blank.
    3. Go round the circle. Each person says one word that shows they know the secret word.
    4. Vote someone out. Innocent? Play another clue round.
    5. Imposter caught? They get one guess at the word. Right: +20 to them. Wrong: +10 to everyone else.
    6. If the imposter survives till only 2 are left, they get +20.
    7. Decoy (optional): one player gets a similar word and doesn't know it. They're on the imposter's side, so the group only wins once both are out.
